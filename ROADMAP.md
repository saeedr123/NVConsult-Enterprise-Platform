# NVConsult Enterprise Platform Roadmap

## P0 — Consolidation & Core
- Import and audit cumulative WordPress theme.
- Preserve current NVConsult visual design.
- Move reusable business logic into `nvconsult-core`.
- Standardize CPTs, taxonomies, relationships and admin fields.
- Complete dynamic homepage section management.

## P1 — Discovery & Conversion
- Countries, services, universities and study programs.
- Scholarships and internships.
- Jobs and recruitment content.
- Search, filters and related-content engine.
- Knowledge Centre and FAQs.

## P2 — Applications & Booking
- Consultation booking.
- Application forms and status tracking.
- Secure document uploads.
- Notifications and email workflows.

## P3 — Portals & CRM
- Student portal.
- Employer portal.
- Agent/consultant workflows.
- Lead and applicant CRM.

## P4 — AI & Automation
- Eligibility assistant.
- CV builder.
- SOP/letter assistance.
- Admin-controlled OpenAI integration with usage limits and auditability.

## P5 — Platform Expansion
- Payments/invoicing.
- Recruitment pipeline integration.
- REST API for mobile clients.
- Multilingual and white-label readiness.
